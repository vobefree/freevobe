How it works

Three steps to your first ship.

1

Download Freevobe

Grab the native app for Windows, macOS or Linux. Nothing else to install — the agent ships with it.

2

Connect Ollama

Freevobe finds localhost:11434, lists your models and shows which ones support tool calling.

3

Start vobing

Describe the app you want. Review every diff, approve commands and let the agent commit.

Gives Back

No ads. A choice to give back.

Freevobe does not sell your attention. Instead of an advertising slot, the app can offer a voluntary reminder about a cause you picked. You give directly to the organisation — Freevobe never handles the money.

No ad network, no tracking, no checkout in the app.

No advertising

Freevobe ships without ad slots, ad scripts and ad networks. Nothing in the app is for sale.

No money through Freevobe

The app has no checkout, no account and no payment provider. Every link opens the organisation’s own page.

Off by default

The reminder in the chat is opt-in, appears in its own block and never touches the agent’s context.

Your log stays local

If you record a contribution, it is marked as self-reported and stored only in your local database.

The catalogue is editorial and links point at each organisation's own front page rather than at a checkout, so a changed payment flow never turns into a broken promise. Freevobe has no partnership, commission or referral agreement with any organisation listed here. Read the full description in docs/gives-back.md.

Privacy

Your code stays yours.

Freevobe runs locally by default. Connect Ollama on your own machine and keep your projects, prompts and files private.

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No telemetry leaving the app.
  • Diff and approval gate before every write.

Data path

  1. Your machine
  2. Ollama
  3. Freevobe
  4. Your project

Everything in that loop runs on hardware you already own. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly connect a cloud model.
